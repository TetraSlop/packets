# 2A Trade cancellation acknowledgement

The other peer's acknowledgement of a [23/00 TrCan](23.md#2300-trcan),
sent back through the server's relay. Its place in the trade is in
[trade.md](../../flows/trade.md).

## 2A/00 TrCanAck

| Direction | Service | Argument | Reply | Body |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P2P | relayed | 0 | none | `struct TrCanAck` |

```c
struct TrCanAck {
};
```

```
P2P GTM0G2A000000@TrCanAck=
```

The body is one empty section. The example is built from the section name;
no capture of it is recorded (unverified). A server relays it only between
a pair allowed by the [peer relay guard](../envelope.md#relay-guard)
and does not answer it.
