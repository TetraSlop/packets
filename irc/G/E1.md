# E1 TeachDV

The client asks for its file locations and for the identities of the
services it addresses next. It is the first `GTM0G` message after login,
sent before the client knows any service identity; the nick it addresses is
in [services.md](../services.md). The reply is
[E2/00 TeachDVAns](E2.md#e200-teachdvans); the place of this exchange in the
startup sequence is in [startup.md](../../flows/startup.md).

## E1/00 TeachDV

| Direction | Service | Argument | Reply | Body |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C2S | service nick it addresses at login (unverified) | 0 | [E2/00 TeachDVAns](E2.md#e200-teachdvans) | none |

```
C2S GTM0GE1000000@TeachDV=
```
