# 12 Rule

The rule query a newly admitted client sends to its table on the
[room service](../services.md#room-service). Its place after admission is
in [tables.md](../../flows/tables.md#sequence).

## 12/00 Rule

| Direction | Service | Argument | Reply | Body |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C2S | room service | 0 | [13/00 Tet and Tab](13.md#1300-tet-and-tab) | none |

```
C2S GTM0G12000000@Rule=
```

The client sends the query only to the table that
admitted it with `GameEA`; before any admission it sends nothing.
