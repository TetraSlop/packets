# 25 Trade rejection

A peer rejects a pending trade invitation. The message travels to the other
peer through the server's relay. Its place in the trade is in
[trade.md](../../flows/trade.md).

## 25/00 Tr

| Direction | Service | Argument | Reply | Body |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P2P | relayed | 0 | none | none |

```
P2P GTM0G25000000@Tr=
```

The body is one empty section named `Tr`, like the invitation on
[21/00](21.md#2100-tr). The example is built from the section name; no
capture of it is recorded (unverified). A server relays it only between a
pair allowed by the [peer relay guard](../envelope.md#relay-guard) and
forgets the invitation between the two peers.
