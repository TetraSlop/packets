# 23 Trade cancellation

A peer cancels a pending trade invitation. The message travels to the other
peer through the server's relay. Its place in the trade is in
[trade.md](../../flows/trade.md).

## 23/00 TrCan

| Direction | Service | Argument | Reply | Body |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P2P | relayed | 0 | [2A/00 TrCanAck](2A.md#2a00-trcanack) | `struct TrCan` |

```c
struct TrCan {
};
```

```
P2P GTM0G23000000@TrCan=
```

The body is one empty section. The example is built from the section name;
no capture of it is recorded (unverified). A server relays it only between
a pair allowed by the [peer relay guard](../envelope.md#relay-guard)
and forgets the invitation between the two peers.
