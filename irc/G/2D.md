# 2D CheckJoinTable

A probe the client sends to a table on the
[room service](../services.md#room-service) before changing to the table's
channel. Spectator entry and Computer entry both send it; the sequences are
in [spectators.md](../../flows/spectators.md),
[match-admission.md](../../flows/match-admission.md#sequence) and
[tables.md](../../flows/tables.md#exit).

## 2D/00 CheckJoinTable

| Direction | Service | Argument | Reply | Body |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C2S | room service | 0 | [2E/00 CheckJoinTable_Ans](2E.md#2e00-checkjointable_ans) | none |

```
C2S GTM0G2D000000@CheckJoinTable=
```

The client sends it before starting the channel
transition: a spectator before its `Req`, a Computer player after
`GameECA`. The normal path does not wait for the reply.
