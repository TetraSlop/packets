# 20 ExitCh

A table channel cleanup message the client sends to the
[room service](../services.md#room-service) only from its recovery path.
The recovery sequence is in
[tables.md](../../flows/tables.md#exit).

## 20/00 ExitCh

| Direction | Service | Argument | Reply | Body |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C2S | room service | 0 | none, or [41/10 GameWa](41.md#4110-gamewa) to the other members | none |

```
C2S GTM0G20000000@ExitCh=
```

The client sends it as the first step of its cleanup
when a [2E CheckJoinTable_Ans](2E.md#2e00-checkjointable_ans) reports a
nonzero `Join` or `Mem`; it then returns to the room channel without waiting
for an answer.
